# Career and OJT Path

## Status

Planning draft. The project is 100% development. This document records the approved direction for employment preparation and post-course field development; it does not promise employment, create an apprenticeship, or authorize production implementation.

## Product purpose

The Career and OJT Path is intended to be a practical Boot Camp differentiator:

> Help a beginner become a stronger entry-level candidate and give an employer a simple plan for continuing that person's supervised field development.

The objective is to help a qualified, prepared beginner reach the front of the interview line. It is not a job-placement service, employer marketplace, registered apprenticeship, licensing program, or employment guarantee.

## Simple learner path

```text
Learn the approved HVAC foundations
  -> reach Interview Ready
  -> begin the job search while continuing Boot Camp
  -> interview honestly as a trained beginner
  -> employer may optionally require course completion
  -> complete Boot Camp
  -> continue supervised field learning with the OJT record and task guides
```

## Milestone 1: Interview Ready

Interview preparation should unlock before course completion, after an approved group of foundational modules. The unlock will be based on required concepts and safety knowledge, not an arbitrary course percentage. The exact boundary will be chosen only after the full course and Unit 1 structures are reviewed.

At this point, the student should be able to:

- explain important beginner HVAC concepts in plain language
- discuss the basic refrigeration cycle, heat, airflow, electricity, tools, and safety at the level actually covered
- describe completed training without overstating field experience
- acknowledge the need for supervised hands-on learning
- communicate professionally with a prospective employer

### Interview Ready toolkit

The first version should include:

- one job-search and interview-preparation lesson
- entry-level HVAC resume guidance and template
- customizable employer introduction letter
- short employer introduction email
- phone, voicemail, and in-person introduction scripts
- common interview questions and approved practice prompts
- questions the student should ask an employer
- interview preparation worksheet and checklist
- follow-up and thank-you message templates
- current enrollment and course-progress summary
- a preview of the OJT Learning Record

The materials should be downloadable and printable. They should also remain usable from the student's phone.

## Applying before completion

Students may begin applying when they reach Interview Ready and continue the course during the job search. All materials must distinguish among:

- currently enrolled
- Interview Ready
- course complete
- field experience completed under employer supervision

No letter, transcript, or interface may imply that an enrolled student has completed Boot Camp or is already qualified for unsupervised technician work.

## Employer-option completion condition

An employer may choose to make Boot Camp completion part of a probationary or development plan. HVAC Training Solutions may provide optional template language such as:

> The employee is expected to complete HVAC Boot Camp by __________ while participating in supervised on-the-job training.

The employer—not HVAC Training Solutions—sets and enforces employment conditions. The template is not an employment contract and must not imply that every employer will accept this arrangement.

## Milestone 2: Course Complete

After the student completes all required modules and soft gates, the platform may unlock:

- course-completion document
- final course-progress summary
- complete OJT Learning Record
- employer cover page explaining how to use the OJT materials
- approved printable field task guides
- current digital field-guide library
- final interview and follow-up materials
- optional request for a mailed completion package

Course completion is not a professional license, EPA certification, NATE certification, registered apprenticeship credential, or proof of unsupervised field competence.

## OJT Learning Record

The OJT Learning Record helps an employer plan and document supervised field development without forcing the employer to invent a training checklist.

Recommended skill stages:

1. **Observed**
2. **Performed under supervision**
3. **Can repeat with normal supervision**

Each approved task may record:

- task or skill
- date
- equipment or system type
- current stage
- supervisor initials or signature
- relevant safety or certification prerequisite
- notes and areas requiring more practice

The record should be short enough for a working supervisor to use. It should not become a burdensome learning-management system for the employer.

## Field task guides

Existing guides such as checking a capacitor or replacing a contactor should be reviewed and placed into one consistent template:

- purpose and scope
- intended supervised audience
- prerequisite knowledge
- hazards, PPE, and energy-control requirements
- tools and materials
- step-by-step procedure
- expected observations or readings where appropriate
- common mistakes
- explicit stop-and-ask-a-supervisor conditions
- supervisor verification where appropriate
- revision date and version

Guides are supervised field references, not authorization for an unqualified person to perform hazardous or regulated work. Electrical energy control, local employer procedures, licensing rules, manufacturer instructions, and EPA Section 608 boundaries remain controlling.

## Digital and mailed delivery

Digital download and phone access are the default first version. The online guide library remains the authoritative source because technical and safety content can be revised.

A mailed package may be piloted manually with a small number of graduates. It may contain:

- personalized completion letter
- course-completion document
- interview workbook
- employer introduction letter
- bound OJT Learning Record
- employer instruction page
- link or QR code to the current digital field guides

Do not automate mailing until the pilot proves that students and employers use the package. Mailing requires address confirmation, fulfillment status, cost and replacement rules, and an address-retention policy.

## Keep V1 intentionally small

V1 does not include:

- employer portal
- public job board
- automatic employer matching
- full applicant-tracking system
- digital supervisor accounts or signatures
- registered-apprenticeship administration
- automated print fulfillment
- elaborate employment agreements
- AI-generated resumes or letters sent without student review

## Acceptance criteria

The Career and OJT Path is ready for a learner pilot when:

- the Interview Ready unlock is tied to approved course concepts
- all templates distinguish enrollment, completion, and field experience accurately
- a student can customize, download, and print the essential materials
- an employer can understand the OJT record without training or an account
- task guides have a consistent safety-reviewed format
- no material promises employment or misstates a credential
- one or two employers and recent students have reviewed the package
- feedback identifies which materials are actually useful

## Decisions still required

- exact Interview Ready module boundary
- fields included in the progress summary
- final resume and letter formats
- exact OJT task list and skill-stage language
- whether the first mailed pilot is included, optional, or separately priced
- whether employer feedback is collected and how consent is handled
