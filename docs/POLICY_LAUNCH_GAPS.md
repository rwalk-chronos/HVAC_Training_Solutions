Checked 2026-09-24. This is a decision list for the marketing cutover, not published legal copy. The Cloudflare development site remains noindex while the existing WordPress site handles live sales.

The development build now includes review pages at `/privacy/`, `/terms/`, and `/refund-policy/`, with `/sqprivacy/` redirecting to `/privacy/` in Cloudflare static assets. These pages provide verified current-course and contact facts, identify unresolved terms plainly, and **are not approved policies for a public launch**. Do not remove the development noindex or route the public domain to this build until the final text and data flows have been approved. The old `/sqprivacy/` export mentions AdSense and the obsolete DoubleClick DART cookie, so copying it would misstate the new site's practices. The export's `/terms/` entry was an unpublished draft for an older property and is not an adopted contract.

The FTC's business guidance says privacy promises must match actual practices and advertising claims must be truthful and supported. These draft pages therefore avoid promises about retention, sale/sharing, course access length, refund processing time, and automatic enrollment that we cannot verify.
